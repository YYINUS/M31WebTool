What we monitor, and what we are adding

The Oct 31 – Nov 6 report missed things because of where its information came from. Below are the four blind spots we found, labelled A to D, then the sources we propose adding. Each source lists the blind spot it fixes.

Four blind spots in last week’s report

A · NO PUSHBACK
We saw no critiques or failed repeats
None of our feeds brings in rebuttals or failed attempts to repeat a result. So readers only see the claim’s side, and every claim looks stronger than it is.
B · TITLES ONLY
3 of 8 claims arrived as titles only
All three came from one journal issue without summaries, so we couldn’t say what they found. We need each paper’s summary (abstract) when it arrives.
C · NOISY SEARCH
559 items matched a keyword but were off-topic
That is 62% of everything we threw away. Searching by research field and journal, instead of by keyword, would cut most of it.
D · MISSING CONTEXT
We don’t follow the mainstream work nearby
Example: we covered a cold fusion article without the government-funded lab results that show what the field has actually measured.

Editorial rules that go with the new sources

No full card without an abstract. Title-only items go to the pending list, unless method alone justifies an action.
Every new claim gets a citation alert the day it opens a thread, so replies reach us without searching.
Every claim is paired with its benchmark, the mainstream result it must be consistent with.
An issue with no live critique source says so at the top, not in the footer.
