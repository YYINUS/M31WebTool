### 1. Birefringence — **WATCH**
- **Field:** cosmology / parity-violating physics (vacuum and speculative physics). **Source:** arXiv preprint (astro-ph.CO), Nov 23, 2026. Submitted to *A&A*.
- **Claim:** Polarisation angles of radio galaxies at z≈0.5 show a rotation that matches the CMB birefringence hint in sign and size.
- **Authors:** Marcus Brüggen, Jorinde Dreismann, Pranjal Trivedi, Shane P. O'Sullivan, Takuya Akahori, Fumihiro Naokawa, Cameron L. Van Eck, Marco Regis et al. Affiliations not in source. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31411
- **Evidence stage: 3.** The fit tests rotation against zero using a population model, and the radio data come from a different team with different systematics. On its own, though, the radio result is not significant, so this is not yet a replication (stage 4).
- **Impact if true: HIGH.** A rotation in the last ~5 Gyr would point to a late-evolving field such as axion-like dark energy. Axion-like dark matter acting before reionisation predicts β≈0 at these redshifts.
- **What it shows:** It uses 33,298 components of 16,649 double radio sources from POSSUM. It adds the χ₀−ψ≈0° population, which halves the variance. Radio only: **β = 0.48° ± 0.35°**, about 1.4σ. Combined with ACT+Planck: β = 0.282° ± 0.056°, 5.0σ.
- **Editorial caution:** The "confirms" in the title is too strong. The 5.0σ combined figure is almost entirely the CMB result: the joint ACT+Planck analysis alone gives 0.277° ± 0.057°, 4.8σ. The radio data are *consistent with* the CMB but do not *confirm* it.
- **Still unknown:** Absolute polarisation-angle calibration of POSSUM, which the authors say is needed. Whether residual Faraday rotation or source morphology systematics can produce ~0.5°.
- **More convincing:** The full POSSUM survey with absolute angle calibration reaching ≥3σ from radio data alone, plus a tomographic split by redshift.
- **Would prove it wrong:** A calibrated full-survey radio β consistent with 0 at ~0.1° precision. That would rule out a late-time rotation, but it would not rule out a rotation before reionisation.
- **Follow:** Marcus Brüggen (lead author); Toshiya Namikawa (radio-galaxy birefringence tomography).
- **Action / trigger:** Read now. Contact only if the POSSUM team publishes an absolute-angle calibration plan or full-survey β with radio-only σ ≤ 0.15°.
- **Compare with:** Joint ACT+Planck β = 0.277° ± 0.057° (https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06480). Earlier radio test using ~3,000 sources (https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39449).

### 2. VLASS SETI — **BENCHMARK**
- **Field:** technosignatures. **Source:** arXiv preprint (astro-ph.SR), Nov 24, 2026. Submitted to AAS Journals.
- **Claim:** There is no convincing broadband 2–4 GHz technosignature from 210,191 Gaia DR3 main-sequence stars within 100 pc, across all VLASS epochs (2017–2026).
- **Author:** Steve Croft (Oxford University / UC Berkeley / SETI Institute). https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31943
- **Evidence stage: 3.** Detections are compared against the expected rate of chance coincidences.
- **Impact if true: MEDIUM.** It sets a new population-wide limit on wideband transmitters. The median per-star limit on steady emission is L_ν < 4.0×10⁸ W Hz⁻¹.
- **Shows:** In the Epoch-1 catalogue, 51 high-reliability positional matches were found, against 1.5 expected by chance. The paper concludes that each is plausibly noise, a background source or stellar emission, and that none warrants priority follow-up.
- **Unknown / check when reading:** How "51 vs 1.5" fits with the statement that the counted detections are "no more numerous than chance". Presumably most matches are explained as ordinary stellar radio emission, which would leave no excess, but the excerpts I saw don't confirm this. The sample also leaves out the faintest late-M and substellar objects.
- **More convincing / wrong:** This is a limit, not a claim. Any future wideband candidate must exceed it.
- **Action:** Store as a benchmark limit for any wideband-signal claim.

### 3. Backreaction — **WATCH**
- **Field:** modified gravity / dark energy alternatives. **Source:** arXiv preprint (astro-ph.CO), Nov 23, 2026.
- **Claim:** DESI's evolving "dark energy" can be reinterpreted as the effect of uneven (inhomogeneous) geometry in ordinary general relativity, with no dark-energy fluid.
- **Author:** Thomas Buchert. Affiliation not in source. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31041
- **Evidence stage: 2.** It uses DESI data to fix the averaged budget, but I saw no head-to-head comparison with a dark-energy model.
- **Impact if true: HIGH.** It would remove the need for dark energy.
- **Shows:** The total geometric budget that replaces dark energy is "pinned five times better" than the split between backreaction and curvature. The author presents this as the backreaction counterpart of DESI's evidence for evolving dark energy.
- **Unknown:** Whether the needed backreaction size is physically achievable. Most N-body/GR studies find it small. The split with curvature is poorly constrained.
- **Would prove it wrong:** Relativistic simulations, or a curvature measurement, showing backreaction far below the needed size.
- **Action / trigger:** No action until a model-comparison paper or relativistic-simulation test appears.
- **Compare with:** Item 4.

### 4. HSC-Y3 shear — **BENCHMARK**
- **Source:** arXiv preprint (astro-ph.CO), Nov 25, 2026. To be submitted to *PRD*. **Authors:** Shreya Mukherjee, Surhud More. Affiliations not in source. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34650
- **Evidence stage: 3.** It is a model comparison on independent lensing data.
- **Shows:**
  - Constant-w model: w = −0.936 (+0.074/−0.067), consistent with a cosmological constant.
  - Time-varying model: w₀ = −0.832, w_a = −1.23, a 2.4σ preference for dynamical dark energy.
  - S₈ ≈ 0.77 in both models.
  - The authors say the dynamical signal is "driven primarily by the geometric distance ladders", not by structure growth.
- **Use:** Any dark-energy-free or modified-gravity explanation (item 3, the running-vacuum papers) must reproduce S₈ ≈ 0.77 as well as the distances.

### 5. Casimir spin-force — **ACT (read and make light contact)**
- **Field:** vacuum / Casimir physics. **Source:** arXiv preprint (hep-ph), Nov 23, 2026. Report numbers KEK-QUP-2026-0020 and KEK-TH-2887 suggest a KEK affiliation; individual affiliations not in source.
- **Claim:** Existing precision Casimir setups could search for axion-mediated spin-dependent forces by comparing reversed polarisations of a magnetically compensated source.
- **Authors:** Xiaolin Ma, Volodymyr Takhistov, Hideo Iizuka. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31378
- **Evidence stage: 1.** This is a proposal with sensitivity projections.
- **Impact if true: MEDIUM.** It would add a new, cleanly falsifiable use for Casimir hardware.
- **Shows:**
  - The spin force reverses sign while the Casimir, gravitational and electrostatic backgrounds "broadly cancel".
  - No shielding is needed, so large probes can work at micrometre gaps.
  - Projected reach on g_p^e g_s^N and g_p^e g_s^e at λ ~ 1–100 µm is "orders of magnitude" beyond existing lab searches.
- **Unknown:** How well the cancellation holds in practice (patch potentials, imperfect magnetic compensation). There is no experimental partner yet.
- **Would prove it wrong:** Systematics in a demonstration run that do not reverse with the polarisation flip and exceed the projected signal.
- **Action / decision rule:** Email Takhistov and ask whether an existing Casimir group has signed on. Scope seed funding only if a named experimental group commits to a demonstration with a pre-specified systematics budget.
- **Context:** Quanta, Nov 25, on new ultralight dark-matter searches (journalism, lead only): https://www.quantamagazine.org/novel-experiments-expand-the-hunt-for-ultralight-dark-matter-20261125/

### 6. UAP radar — **CONTEXT**
- **Source:** DoD PURSUE release 8, Nov 20 (one day before the window), covered by EarthSky on Nov 22. File DOW-UAP-D165, "Unresolved Radar Detection North America 2023".
- **Links:** https://www.war.gov/UFO/?releaseDate=Release+08&release=08 · https://earthsky.org/human-world/pentagon-uap-files-part-8-radar-track-analysis/
- **Evidence stage: 2.** Sensor data with official analysis, but no comparison case.
- **Shows:**
  - A civil air-surveillance radar recorded 8 detections over 84 s at an estimated 55,000–70,000 ft.
  - If one object made them, it moved at about 1,650 mph (~Mach 2.5) and changed bearing by ~39°.
  - The analysis itself says the 12-second sweep interval cannot establish that the detections are one object or a continuous track.
- **What would help:** Raw radar returns, data from other sensors at the same time, and a check of false-track rates for that radar.
- **Action:** None. Revisit only if a release includes multi-sensor raw data. Release 8 has 43 files (40 documents, 3 videos), mostly mixed old and new material.

### 7. Cold fusion essay — **CONTEXT**
- **Source:** Essay by Huw Price, *3 Quarks Daily*, Nov 26, 2026: https://3quarksdaily.com/3quarksdaily/2026/11/cold-fusion-what-musser-misses.html
- **Evidence stage: 0.**
- **What it is:** A reply to George Musser's Quanta pieces on cold fusion (https://www.quantamagazine.org/is-there-anything-left-in-the-dream-of-cold-fusion-20261102/). Price argues that recent work shows many 1989 critics were wrong. He also cites Metzler et al., who note that far more than a 10¹⁸ enhancement would be needed for measurable excess heat.
- **Compare with:** *Nature Communications*, "Enhanced nuclear fusion in the sub-keV energy regime" (2026): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74421-1. It reports materials-dependent enhancement of low-energy fusion in solids, but says such fusion rates are not presently relevant for energy production.
- **Action:** Read for framing only. The gap between "enhanced cross-sections" and "excess heat" is the key benchmark for any LENR proposal.
